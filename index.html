Every restaurant overpreps. The question is how much.

The margin between 'just right' and 'too much' is often just 5 or 10 portions. That's not a big number. But over a week, a month, a year, it adds up. deitalite learns your patterns and tells you what to expect before you start prepping.

Built from your data

A year of what you sold and when you sold it. That's all we need. From that, we learn how your place responds to weather, holidays, busy weekends, quiet Mondays. Not a general guess. A forecast built around your restaurant specifically. Ready from day one.

Your menu can change. The forecast keeps up.

You don't need years of history for every dish. You need it for your restaurant. We already know your busy days, your slow days, how weather affects you. A new dish just needs a couple of weeks of sales to find its place. That's enough.
