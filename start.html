Apply for the pilot program

We're looking for independent restaurants to help us refine deitalite before wider launch. Tell us about your restaurant and we'll be in touch.

Limited spots · Priority support · Shape the product

What pilot restaurants get

  • Daily prep forecasts: item-level quantities for the next 7 days, delivered at 6am.
  • Weather and events built in: we track what affects your city so you don't have to.
  • Direct feedback channel: tell us what works, what doesn't, what you need.
  • Priority support: we'll help you get set up and answer questions quickly.
  • Special terms: founding restaurants get preferred pricing when we launch.
  • No long-term commitment: try it, see if it helps. No pressure to continue.

What we need from you

  • Sales data from your POS: 12 months of item-level sales (CSV or Excel). We'll help you export.
  • Your location: so we connect the right weather and event data.
  • Willingness to give feedback: we're building this together.

How it works

1

Apply below

Tell us about your restaurant: location, type, and how weather affects your business.

No commitment required

Quick questions

What if I can't export from my POS?

We'll help. After you apply, we send instructions for common POS systems. If yours isn't listed, email us and we'll figure it out together.

What does the pilot cost?

We'll discuss terms based on your needs. Pilot restaurants get special pricing in exchange for feedback.

How long until I can use it?

1 business day after you upload your sales data. Most restaurants are checking their prep plan by the next morning.
